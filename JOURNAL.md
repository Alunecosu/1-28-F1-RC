# 2026-11-19: Addition to the last devlog

**Total time spent: 40m**

# 2026-10-31: Overall rigidity

**Total time spent: 44m**

## Suspension reinforcement

After printing the Front suspension I noticed it was too weak, the top part of it broke off when I tried to take off the supports therefore I added a whole new arm on top and added filets around the weak spots.

![image.png](https://cdn.hackclub.com/01a1916e-e67c-7608-a75d-57a144e97478/image.png)

## Tires reinforcement & rims redesign

After I printed the tires I found out they don't fit and the top and bottom layers just shred off so I redesigned the outer shape of the rims and made the tires thicker.

# 2026-10-17: Front wheel hub

**Total time spent: 1.5 hours**

I built the front wheel hubs, unfortunately I had to ditch the front suspension for smooth steering (this might be upgraded in the future if shoulder screws fit in the budget) I am using two MR62 bearings inside the kingpin spinning on a M2x16 (will probably switch to a M2x14 but that depends on the budget) to achieve smooth rotation, therefore no threads touch the printed part.
The rims also use two MR62 which rotate on a M2x12 screw.

# 2026-10-11: Final Differential

**Total time spent: 3 hours**

I changed to a 3 spider gears design and glues the pins that hold them inside the spur, the result was 0 wobble even under 10K RPM. The print went smooth and fast as I only had to print the spur and 1 additional spider gear.

I added a little bit of lubricant oil and the efficiency of the drivetrain doubled, the drivetrain moves freely under almost no force, the high rpm 130 motor managed to move it at 0.6V using 0.1A.

# 2026-10-09: Printed Differential

**Total time spent: 4 hours**

The first print of the differential was a failure.
They had some small clearance issues which I later on fixed. The 2 spider gears design was wobbly and couldn't balance the spur straight onto the shaft so I decided to redesign it to have 3 spider gears instead of 2 (**check image 3**).

The differential assembly was printed at 0.06mm layer height on a 0.2mm nozzle to achieve maximum accuracy, which is where a lot of time went away.
